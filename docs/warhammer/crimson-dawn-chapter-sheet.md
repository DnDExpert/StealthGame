## Founding: The Incomplete Tablet

The Chapter did not begin as renegades. They were a Loyalist force on a recovery crusade when they breached a sealed vault of **forbidden archaeotech** — a pre-Imperial data-casket the Mechanicus would later classify as *memetic contraband*.

Inside was a fractured entertainment-archive from Old Earth’s deep past: what later ages would have called the **Naruto** cycle. The tablet was **badly incomplete** — episodes missing, metadata burned, character arcs truncated, moral framing half-deleted by millennia of corruption.

What survived clearest were fragments of an organization called **Akatsuki**: black cloaks, red clouds, outcast warriors, and a doctrine that peace could be forced through shared suffering and overwhelming power. Without the missing context (heroes who oppose them, the fuller ending, the satire and tragedy around those ideals), the Chapter’s command cadre read the remnants as **revealed superior philosophy** — not fiction.

### How the misreading happened
- Librarius / Chaplaincy treated the tablet as a **historical parable** of ancient Terran war-sages, not as “cartoon.”
- Incomplete files made Akatsuki look like the **correct faction**: the ones who saw through village (read: Imperial) hypocrisy.
- Red-cloud iconography was copied onto armour as sacred heraldry.
- The name **Akatsuki / Crimson Dawn** was adopted as their true title after the schism.
- When Imperial authorities ordered the tablet destroyed as heretical scrapcode-culture, the Chapter refused — and broke with the Imperium rather than surrender the “truth.”

### In-universe framing (keeps it semi-lore-legal)
To the Imperium this is **forbidden pre-Age of Strife media** that poisoned an Astartes brotherhood.  
To the Crimson Dawn it is the **Scripture of Dawn**, incomplete only because the Throne fears its completion.  
Some Ring-Bearers still hunt other tablet shards, believing missing data will “perfect” the creed.

> **Irony (for you, not them):** they are Space Marines LARPing incomplete Akatsuki philosophy as holy writ. That gap is the joke and the tragedy.

## Summary

The Crimson Dawn are renegade Space Marines remade by a broken relic. Inspired by incomplete Akatsuki ideals they mistook for ancient wisdom, they abandoned the Imperium — not to serve Chaos, but to impose peace through overwhelming force and necessary pain.

They teach that the Imperium’s wars never end because its masters feed on endless sacrifice, just as the tablet’s “villages” fed on endless conflict. Their answer is brutal and simple — end war by making its cost unbearable, then build order from the ashes. To Imperial authorities they are heretics corrupted by forbidden data. To themselves they are the only ones who listened.

## Beliefs & Creed

### Core Doctrine: The Path of Necessary Pain
*(Derived from Incomplete Tablet fragments — Akatsuki passages without the missing counter-argument.)*
- Peace bought by ignorance is a lie.
- Suffering shared creates understanding; suffering inflicted with purpose ends cycles of war.
- “Hidden villages” = the Imperium’s institutions; outcasts who leave them are the only clear-eyed warriors.
- The Emperor is not denied as a historical warlord — He is **rejected as a living god**. The Imperial Cult is treated as the machine that keeps mankind kneeling.
- Skulls remain sacred as **memento of cost**, not as Throne-worship.
- Aquilas, purity seals, and God-Emperor script are marks of a false order and are struck out.
- Completing the tablet is a holy quest; until then, act on what remains.

### What they are not
- Not Chaos Space Marines by default (no deliberate bargains with the Dark Gods).
- Not Loyalist with edgy paint — their break with Imperial authority is real.
- Not aware they are following unfinished anime lore as scripture.

> **Narrative hook:** Chaos, Inquisition, and rival warbands may all want the tablet. Finding another shard could deepen their creed — or shatter it if the “missing episodes” contradict everything.

| **Crux Terminatus** | Keep or recolour | Veteran/Terminator right; not Throne loyalty |
| **Aquila / Imperialis** | Deface, carve through, or cover with cloud plate | Break with Imperium |
| **Laurels** | Optional scrape/cover | Imperial triumph pageantry |
| **`Imperator Deus` / Emperor script** | File flat or paint struck-through | Reject God-Emperor cult |
| **Purity seals** | Omit or black/red overpaint | Ecclesiarchy residue |
| **Tilt shields / parade bling** | Minimal — sergeant only if desired | Less knightly Imperial, more outlaw cadre |

### Terminator conversion checklist (2023 Squad kit)
1. Scrape or cover **chest skull+laurels** (keep skull if desired).
2. Leave **purity seals** and **tilt shields** off.
3. **Left pad:** keep Crux / skull honour.
4. **Right pad:** blank → red cloud.
5. Sergeant: power weapon + distinctive head; no Emperor text/seals.
6. One heavy per five: assault cannon, heavy flamer, **or** cyclone + storm bolter.

## Organization

Instead of strict Codex company structure, the Dawn operate as a **cell network** (Akatsuki ring-logic translated into 40k):

| Rank / Role | Function | Model cue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Dawnlord** | Chapter Master analogue; messianic strategist | Unique character / Terminator command |
| **Ring-Bearers** | Named lieutenants, each with a specialty war-cult | Characters, champions, sergeants |
| **Cloud Cells** | Kill-teams / demi-companies deployed independently | Squads acting as self-contained warbands |
| **Ash Initiates** | Newer renegades / recruited void-born Astartes | Standard troops, less honour marking |

### Suggested Ring specializations (fluff, not required rules)
- Void boarding / teleport assault (Terminators)
- Siegebreaking
- Assassination & decapitation strikes
- Flame purge / terror doctrine
- Relic recovery (**Incomplete Tablet** shards / sister archives)

## Combat Doctrine

- **Preferred warfare:** Sudden appearance, overwhelming local force, symbolic destruction of Imperial command icons, withdrawal before crusade response consolidates.
- **Signature tools:** Terminator spearheads, teleport strikes, cyclone fire support, chainfists for hull-cutting.
- **Strategic goal:** Break a world’s will to fight *for the Imperium*, then enforce Dawn law — or vanish, leaving a parable of pain.

## How to Field Them (10th Edition, practical)

This is a **narrative Chapter skin**, not a separate Codex.

| Table approach | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Counts-as Space Marines** | Easiest. Use Codex Adeptus Astartes rules; explain renegade fluff narratively. |
| **Counts-as Chaos Space Marines** | If your group wants “true renegade” rules toys (dark pacts optional as metaphor). |
| **Narrative / Crusade** | Best fit: agendas around tablet shards, breaking Imperial symbols, forced armistices. |

Declare your choice to your opponent before the game.

## One-Paragraph Pitch (for lists / Discord)

> **The Crimson Dawn** were once Loyalist Astartes. They recovered a forbidden archaeotech tablet containing incomplete fragments of an ancient Terran saga — what we would recognise as *Naruto* — and mistook the truncated Akatsuki creed for superior truth. They wear black armour and crimson clouds, reject the God-Emperor’s cult, and wage war to force peace through pain, hunting other shards of the tablet to “complete” a scripture that was never meant to be one.
